People ask why I didn't just use Hugo or Jekyll. Because I want to own the machine.

Writing a static site generator in Erlang is a lesson in pattern matching. Using binaries like `inline code` feels like soldering wires together. It's precise, it's fast, and it doesn't have 9,000 npm dependencies. 

Functional programming isn't just for academics; it's for people who hate debugging state.
