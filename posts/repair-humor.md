Parfois, on répare un vieil ampli et on réalise que le problème n'est pas le circuit, mais l'utilisateur. ⚡

I found a 1970s receiver where the "owner" had replaced a fuse with a literal gum wrapper. It’s a miracle the house didn't burn down. C'est vraiment du "punk rock" mais de la pire façon possible. No amount of DeoxIT can fix a total lack of common sense. 🛠️ logic gates shouldn't be subjected to this kind of abuse, but here we are, soldering the pieces back together in the dark.
