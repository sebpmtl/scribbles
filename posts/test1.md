
# The Art of Doing Nothing: A Guide to Ultimate Productivity

In today's fat paced world, we are often told to hustle harder. But what if the secret to success is actually... **nothing**?

## Why "Nothing" is Everything
Sometimes, stepping away from the super screen is the best way to solve a complex problem. Research (that I totally didn't make up) shows that sitting in silence for 15 minutes a day can:

* Increase creative thinking
* Reduce screen-induced headaches
* Make you appreciate your coffee break more

> "Doing nothing often leads to the very best of something." — *A.A. Milne*
